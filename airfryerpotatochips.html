Air Fryer Sweet Potato Chips

Potato Chips that can be made with the use of an air fryer!

Ingredients

  • Sweet Potato
  • Canola Oil (1 tbsp)
  • Sea Salt (1 tsp)
  • Black Pepper
  • Cooking Spray

Steps

  1. Slice Potato so rounds are 1/16 inch thick
  2. Soak slices in water for 20 mins; dry potatoes and bowl when finished
  3. Preheat air fryer to 350ºF (175ºC)
  4. Use cooking spray to lightly coat air fryer basket
  5. Arrange potato slices in even layer inside basket
  6. Cook until golden and crispy (~14-16 minutes)
  7. When finished cooking, let cool for 5 minutes
  8. Store chips in an airtight container for 3 days
